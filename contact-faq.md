## FAQ

### Who is Haney?

Haney is an apartment cat fed by Sakthivel T.

She is a female calico cat and the inspiration behind the project's name.

While she has never written Python code, she has supervised numerous coding sessions.

---

### Is Haney open source?

Yes.

Haney is open source and can be modified, extended, and tailored for individual or organizational use cases.

---

### Do you collect data?

No.

Haney does not collect user data.

Project data remains in your local `.haney` directory.

No analytics.

No telemetry.

No data harvesting.

No selling data.

---

### Why should I use Haney?

Use Haney if you value:

* Transparency
* Local project awareness
* Control over providers and models
* Simple configuration
* Open-source tooling

If those things are important to you, Haney may be a good fit.

---

### Why are only a few providers supported?

The project is still evolving.

Additional providers and integrations may be added in future releases.

---

### Why can't I log in using my AI subscription account?

Direct subscription-based authentication is not currently supported.

This functionality may be implemented in a future release.

---

### How was Haney tested?

Haney has primarily been tested using:

* GPT-5.4
* DeepSeek

Testing coverage will continue to improve as the project evolves.

---

### What if I encounter issues?

Please use the contact form on the [Contact page](/contact) and describe the issue as precisely as possible.

Helpful information includes:

* What happened
* Steps to reproduce the issue
* Error messages
* Screenshots (if available)

Support requests should be related specifically to Haney Coding Agent.

---

### How can I help?

The easiest way to help is:

1. Use Haney.
2. Build something with it.
3. Share feedback.
4. Tell others if you find it useful.

If you would like to contribute code, documentation, ideas, testing, or other forms of support, feel free to get in touch.

This is an evolving project and community contributions are welcome.
